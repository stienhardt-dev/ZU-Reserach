Executive Summary

Zendar Universe (ZU) occupies a unique niche: a startup blending real astronomy research (Kusmitha Bonda, Arezoo Shirmohammadi) with creative storytelling (comics, characters, universe lore). The 4 dummy researchers — Dr. Mateo Castillo, Dr. Jean-Luc Moreau, Dr. Lars Eriksen, and Dr. Elena Vance — currently present as original research authors, which creates brand risk as ZU scales.

The Strategy

Reposition these 4 personas as Senior Research Analysts & Science Communicators who curate breakthrough research from global institutions, provide expert commentary, explore future possibilities, and make complex astrophysics accessible. This model mirrors successful platforms like Astrobites, PBS Space Time, and Quanta Magazine — all of which have built massive authority without conducting original research.

Expected Impact

  • Sustainable brand credibility that withstands scrutiny
  • 10x content velocity (curating is faster than publishing original research)
  • SEO dominance on trending astronomy topics within 6-12 months
  • Clear differentiation between ZU's original research (real researchers) and ZU's research communication arm (dummy researchers)

9.2 On-Page SEO Checklist

  • SEO title: 50-60 characters, primary keyword first
  • Meta description: 150-160 characters, includes CTA, primary + secondary keyword
  • URL slug: short, keyword-rich, hyphenated
  • H1: matches SEO title (one H1 per page)
  • H2/H3 hierarchy: includes question-based keywords
  • Internal links: minimum 3 links to other ZU content
  • External links: 1-2 links to authoritative sources (NASA, ESA, arXiv)
  • Image alt text: descriptive, keyword-inclusive
  • First 100 words: contains primary keyword
  • TL;DR/summary: appears in first 30% of content (AI citation optimization)
  • FAQ section: 3-5 questions with concise answers
  • Author byline: links to researcher profile page
  • Related publications: 3-5 links to related ZU content
  • Word count: minimum 1,500 words for research analyses
  • Reading time: displayed on page
